My First Blog

Starting my journey in web development has been one of the most exciting steps in my learning path. Writing simple HTML pages taught me the foundation of how the web works.

At first, seeing plain text on a blank screen didn’t seem interesting, but when I realized that every website starts this way, it motivated me to continue building, one tag at a time. Each new element feels like unlocking a new creative tool!

I’m eager to move ahead into CSS and JavaScript next to make my pages beautiful and interactive.
